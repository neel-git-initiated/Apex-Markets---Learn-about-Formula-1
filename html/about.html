ABOUT APEX MARKETS

Formula 1 is more than speed. It is a combination of engineering, strategy, teamwork, and split-second decisions. Apex Markets makes these concepts easier to understand through structured lessons and visual learning.

Our goal is to help beginners understand the sport behind the spectacle, while introducing them to a weather-based podium prediction experience.

What Drives Us?

01. Learn the Sport

Understand F1 fundamentals, racing rules, circuits, tyres, and pit-stop strategies.

02. Think Strategically

Explore how weather and racing decisions can influence a Grand Prix.

03. Learn Visually

Discover complex concepts through organized lessons, images, and clear explanations.

04. Predict the Podium

Experience a simple weather-based model that predicts the top three drivers.

Our Mission

To make Formula 1 knowledge accessible to everyone and turn curious viewers into informed fans.
