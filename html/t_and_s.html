Know Your Tyres

Every tyre choice changes the race. Hover over a tyre to discover its purpose.

Soft

Maximum Grip

Soft tyres offer strong grip and are usually faster over a short run, but tend to wear out more quickly.

Medium

Balanced Choice

Medium tyres balance grip and durability, making them a versatile option for different race strategies.

Hard

Built to Last

Hard tyres generally last longer but offer less grip than softer compounds in comparable conditions.

Full Wet

Rain Specialist

Full Wet tyres are designed for very wet conditions, helping disperse water and reduce the risk of aquaplaning.

Radio 📢 : BOX BOX

Every second counts in Formula 1. A pit crew changes all four tyres in just a few seconds, helping drivers get back on track quickly. A slow pit stop can cost valuable positions, while a fast one can change the outcome of a race.

Undercut vs Overcut

UNDER
CUT

Pit earlier than your rival and use fresh tyres to gain time before they make their stop.

OVER
CUT

Stay on track longer than your rival and use strong lap times to gain a strategic advantage.
